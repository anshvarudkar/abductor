🌍 Abductor - Planet Backgrounds 🌎

Starter planet. Lush forests, cities, and civilization. Perfect for a growing UFO.
Level 1-3 • Abduct: Trees, People, Cars, Buildings

Barren gray landscape with craters. Low gravity means objects float longer!

Red rocky desert with dust storms. Ancient structures and research stations.
Level 7-9 • Abduct: Colonies, Rovers, Terraformers
🟡 Venus
Toxic yellow atmosphere with extreme heat. Volcanic and dangerous!
Level 10-12 • Abduct: Research Probes, Floating Stations
🔵 Neptune
Deep blue gas giant with swirling storms and fierce winds.
Level 13-15 • Abduct: Gas Mining Stations, Storm Harvesters
🌌 Deep Space
The void between worlds. Asteroids, space stations, and cosmic wonders.
Level 16+ • Abduct: Planets, Stars, Galaxies, Universes
